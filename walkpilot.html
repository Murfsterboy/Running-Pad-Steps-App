Import & export

How to get walks from Garmin, Strava and othersTCX or GPX files
Garmin Connect: open the activity on connect.garmin.com → ⚙ (top right) → Export to TCX.
Strava: open the activity on strava.com → ⋯ → Export GPX.
Other apps: most can export TCX or GPX. Save the file, then tap Import walks and choose it (you can pick several).

Imported walks count towards This week, Distance, Streak and Active minutes. Walks that overlap one already in History are skipped, so nothing is counted twice.

Sending a WalkPilot walk the other way: tap a walk below → Export TCX, then upload it at connect.garmin.com → Import Data, or strava.com → Upload. Set the activity type to Treadmill walk or Walk after uploading. If your watch recorded the same walk, don't upload it – it would be counted twice.

Enter where each zone starts (bpm), exactly as Garmin shows. Each zone ends 1 bpm below the next; Zone 5 ends at your max HR.

About WalkPilot

WalkPilot v35 – a free walking pad coach: step targets, heart-rate zone workouts, live graphs and history.

Works with: walking pads and treadmills that support standard Bluetooth fitness control (FTMS) – including many UREVO models and other brands that work with Kinomap or Zwift. Needs Chrome on Android (or desktop Chrome/Edge). iPhone browsers don't support Bluetooth.

Privacy: everything stays on your phone. WalkPilot has no account and sends no data anywhere. Use Backup & restore in Settings to keep a copy.

Safety: WalkPilot is not a medical device and gives no medical advice. Heart-rate zones are estimates. Check with your GP before starting hard exercise, especially if you have a heart, blood-pressure or other health condition. Keep the pad's safety key attached and stop if you feel unwell.

Not affiliated with or endorsed by UREVO, Garmin or any equipment maker. Use at your own risk.
