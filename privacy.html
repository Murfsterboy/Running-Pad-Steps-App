Privacy policy and terms of use · last updated 26 October 2026

Privacy policy

In short: your data stays on your phone. WalkPilot has no accounts, no adverts and no analytics, and we don't collect, sell or share your personal data.

What WalkPilot stores, and where

  • Your settings and answers from setup (age, sex, height, weight, health check answers, goals), your walks, heart-rate readings, fitness results and check-ins.
  • All of this is stored only on your device, in the app's own storage. It is never sent to us or to anyone else.
  • Removing the app, or clearing its data in your browser or phone settings, deletes it. Use Backup if you want a copy.

Bluetooth

WalkPilot uses Bluetooth only to connect to your walking pad and, if you choose, a heart-rate watch. It remembers which devices you connected so it can reconnect them.

Files you choose to import, export or back up

Imported walk files (TCX/GPX) and backups are read on your device. Files you export or save are created on your device; where they go next (for example Google Drive, Garmin Connect or Strava) is your choice and covered by those services' own privacy policies.

Health apps (Android app)

If you allow it, the Android app can read and write walking data with Health Connect. It only reads and writes the data types you approve, uses them only inside WalkPilot, and you can withdraw permission at any time in Health Connect.

Purchases (Android app)

WalkPilot Pro is bought through Google Play. Google handles the payment; we receive no card details.

Pad check reports

A Pad check report contains your pad's name and technical details and your phone's browser version – nothing personal. It is only shared if you choose to send it.

Children

WalkPilot is intended for adults and isn't directed at children under 13.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete – you control all of it on your device. If you have any questions, contact us at walkpilot@outlook.com.

Terms of use

  • Not a medical device. WalkPilot gives general wellness information. Heart-rate zones, VO2 max, fitness age, calories and other figures are estimates, not medical measurements or advice.
  • Check with your GP first if you have a heart condition, high blood pressure, chest pain, dizziness, a joint problem, are pregnant, take medicine that affects your heart rate, or have any other health concern.
  • Exercise safely. WalkPilot can change your pad's speed and incline automatically. Keep the safety key attached, start slowly, keep children and pets away from the belt, and stop straight away if you feel dizzy, breathless, in pain or unwell. In an emergency call 999.
  • You use WalkPilot and your walking pad at your own risk. Follow your pad maker's instructions and limits.
  • WalkPilot is an independent app and is not made by, or affiliated with, UREVO, Garmin, Google, Apple or any walking pad maker. Product names belong to their owners.
  • We may update WalkPilot and these terms. The date at the top shows the latest version.
