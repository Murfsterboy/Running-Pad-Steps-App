WalkPilot Pad Scanner

Finds out whether your walking pad sends its own step count over Bluetooth. Your WalkPilot app and data are not affected.

How to run the test (about 3 minutes)

  1. Close WalkPilot and the pad's own app. Switch the pad on, standing still.
  2. Tap Connect & scan and pick your pad.
  3. Type the pad's step count now (0 if just switched on), then start walking with the pad's remote at a steady pace.
  4. Walk for 2–3 minutes – the timer below counts up.
  5. Stop walking, type the pad's step count at the end, tap Finish, then Copy report and paste it into the chat with Claude.
